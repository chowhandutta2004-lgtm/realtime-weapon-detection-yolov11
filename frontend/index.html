Detectable Threats

Handgun

Pistols, revolvers, and compact firearms detected with bounding box filters to minimize false positives from everyday objects.

Long Gun

Rifles, shotguns, and other long-barreled firearms. The model accounts for larger bounding box ratios typical of elongated weapons.

Knife

Bladed weapons including kitchen knives, combat knives, and machetes. Tuned with a lower confidence threshold for sharper recall, even at distance.

Explosive

Grenades, IEDs, and explosive devices. Trained on military and improvised ordnance samples to catch high-risk threats early.

How it works
01

Capture

Open your webcam, plug in an IP camera, or drag-and-drop any image or video file. ShieldAI accepts JPG, PNG, WebP, MP4, AVI, MOV, and MKV.

02

Detect

Every frame is processed through YOLOv11s with per-class confidence thresholds, bounding box dimension filters, and optional quadrant-crop scanning for knives at distance.

03

Alert

Detected threats are highlighted with color-coded bounding boxes, logged in the alerts panel with cropped evidence, and confidence scores are displayed in real-time.

Notes

Accuracy Improves with Training

The detection model is powered by YOLOv11s and gets significantly more precise with additional training cycles. More epochs, better data, and fine-tuning on domain-specific samples directly translate to sharper and more reliable predictions in real-world scenarios.

Open to Collaborations

If you're working on computer vision, security systems, or AI-driven surveillance and want to collaborate — let's connect. Whether it's contributing training data, improving the model architecture, or integrating this into a larger pipeline, I'm always open to building together.

Reducing False Positives

The model currently needs more exposure to negative samples — everyday objects like air conditioners, refrigerators, bookshelves, shadows, and other household items that can occasionally be misidentified as threats. Training on these hard negatives will help the model distinguish real weapons from benign objects with much higher confidence.
